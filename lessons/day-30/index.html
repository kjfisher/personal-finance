15min · 5–20

Mini-lesson: 401(k), match, IRA & Roth

Your 3% + employer 3% = 6% of your pay into your 401(k).

  • Slides: 401(k), IRA vs. Roth IRA.
  • Board math: $50,000 salary × 3% = $1,500 from you + $1,500 match = $3,000 a year.
  • Traditional = pre-tax now, taxed in retirement. Roth = taxed now, qualified withdrawals tax-free.
  • Misconception: "The match is optional money I can skip." Skipping it turns down free money.
15min · 20–35

Social Security forecast

Estimate your monthly benefit at 62.

  • Slides: Social Security (6.2% employee + 6.2% employer). Guided Notes #11–12 with the SSA Quick Calculator.
  • Students under 21 adjust their birth date (the calculator needs age 21+). Use their Unit 1 career salary.
25min · 35–60

401(k) compound interest tool

How big can 6% a month grow by 62?

  • Guided Notes #13–15 on investor.gov: initial $0, monthly = 6% of gross monthly pay, years = 62 − graduation age, rate = historical market return, compounded annually.
  • Then #16–19: years after 62 × 12 = months; 401(k) total ÷ months; add Social Security = monthly retirement income.
  • Example on the board: $633,421.96 ÷ 180 months = $3,519.01 a month.
