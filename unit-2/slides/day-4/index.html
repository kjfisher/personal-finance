Two words to know

Gross vs. net

Gross pay

Everything you earn before anything is taken out. The number on the job offer.

Net pay

Take-home pay: what's left after taxes and other deductions.

Gross pay math · Notes box 3

Yearly salary ÷ the number of paychecks

  • Monthly: ÷ 12
  • Every two weeks (biweekly): ÷ 26
  • Weekly: ÷ 52
Worked example

$54,600 a year

Monthly (÷ 12)$4,550
Biweekly (÷ 26)$2,100
Weekly (÷ 52)$1,050
