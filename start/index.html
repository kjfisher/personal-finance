Where things live

  • Daily Agenda: today's plan. Pick your blocks (1 & 3 or 5 & 7) so the dates match, then tap Today.
  • Unit and lesson pages (from the agenda): the learning target, slides, guided notes, key vocabulary and that unit's games.
  • Glossary: every key word from Units 1–10, with flashcards.
  • My Targets: rate yourself on every "I can" target and get relearn links before a quiz or a reassessment.
  • Resources: videos, calculators and trusted sites for every unit.
  • Passport: stamps and badges for the games you've played.
  • Arcade: practice games for every unit, the midterm, the final and case studies.

Unit case studies

Each unit case study is its own 4-point grade: Claim, Calculate, Evidence, Advice, each worth 0, 0.5 or 1. Practice with Make the Call and Grade It.

Families

Personal Finance covers the money skills every adult uses: compounding, paychecks and pay stubs, budgeting, federal taxes, banking, credit, buying a car and a home, insurance, and investing and retirement. It ends with a final case study where students advise a client and back every recommendation with a number.

Easy ways to help at home

  • Look at a real pay stub together and find gross pay, net pay and FICA.
  • Ask your student to explain the 50/30/20 budget, or credit utilization, in their own words.
  • Share a money decision you made (a car, insurance, a savings goal) and how you thought it through.
  • Ask your student to teach you one word from the Glossary.

Questions about grades or progress: please contact the teacher through the school's usual channels.

Substitute teachers

  1. Open the Daily Agenda, pick the class's blocks, and tap Today.
  2. Open that day's lesson page. It has the learning target and a timed plan for each part of the block.
  3. Project the slides with ▶ Today's slides (or Projector mode). Students follow along in their guided notes.
  4. Start with the warm-up QR code and end with the exit ticket QR code. Students show their result screen on the way out.

Need a backup plan? Open the Arcade, filter to the current unit, and play a class game on the board (Showdown or Corners), or have students play that unit's solo games on their iPads.

Leave quizzes and case studies for the teacher unless you were given them directly.
