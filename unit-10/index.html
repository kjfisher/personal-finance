Key vocabulary

Financial planYour money map: what you earn, spend, save, invest and protect, with numbers.
Take-home (net) payWhat lands in your account after taxes, insurance and 401(k). Every plan starts here.
Effective tax rateTotal tax ÷ salary. Always lower than your top bracket.
Emergency fund3 months of needs, saved in a high-yield savings account.
Employer matchFree money your employer adds to your 401(k) when you contribute.
Stress testRunning your plan through a bad month (job loss, big repair) to see if it holds.
Beneficiary / executorWho gets an account when you die / who carries out your will.
CERClaim, evidence, reasoning: how you explain your 3 best decisions.
