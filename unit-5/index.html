Key vocabulary

Checking accountFor everyday money: direct deposit, debit card, bills. Usually little or no interest.
Savings accountFor money you're setting aside. Earns interest.
High-yield savings account (HYSA)A savings account, often at an online bank, that pays much more interest.
CD (certificate of deposit)Money locked for a set term at a fixed rate; early withdrawal = penalty.
APYAnnual percentage yield: the yearly return including compounding.
FDIC / NCUAInsure deposits up to $250,000 per depositor, per bank (FDIC) or credit union (NCUA).
Credit unionA not-for-profit financial cooperative owned by its members.
Routing / account numberRouting = which bank (9 digits). Account number = your account.
Debit card vs. credit cardDebit spends your own checking money. Credit borrows money you must repay.
OverdraftSpending more than your balance; banks may charge a fee (often about $35).
Minimum balanceThe amount you must keep to avoid a monthly fee.
Compound interest formulaA = P(1 + r)^t: same answer as working a CD year by year.
