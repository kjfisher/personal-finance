    Ready to become a Future Millionaire?

    The whole class (or one team at a time) climbs 15 questions from $100 to $1,000,000. Questions cover all four midterm units and get harder as the money goes up.

    • Pick an answer (click it or press A–D), then lock it with Final answer (or Enter).
    • A wrong answer is a strike. You still see the explanation and keep climbing. 3 strikes ends the game and you keep your last safe haven.
    • Three lifelines, once each: 50:50, a Notes hint, and a 30-second Table huddle.
    • At the end you'll see which unit cost you the most. That's the unit to study tonight.
