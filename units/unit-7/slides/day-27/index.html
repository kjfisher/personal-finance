Depreciation · Notes box 3

A new car loses value fastest at the start

Illustration only: real depreciation depends on the make, model, miles and condition

Illustration: a $30,000 new car might be worth about $24,000 after 1 year, $18,000 after 3 and $13,500 after 5. A car is transportation, not an investment.

Warranties · Notes box 4

Basic vs. powertrain

Basic (bumper-to-bumper)

Covers most parts: electronics, AC, power windows. Often about 3 years or 36,000 miles.

Powertrain

Covers the engine, transmission and drive parts. Often about 5 years or 60,000 miles.

Maintenance

Budget for keeping it running

  • Oil changes, tires, brakes, inspections
  • Used cars past warranty: keep repair money in your emergency fund

Common mistakes · Notes box 4

Car-buying traps

  • "What monthly payment do you want?" Answer with an out-the-door price instead.
  • A salvage title = the car was totaled once. Big red flag.
  • A new car is transportation, not an investment: it depreciates fast.
