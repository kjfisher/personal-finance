Ready to buy? · Notes box 2

Signs you're ready

  • A steady job (often 2+ years)
  • An emergency fund AND a down payment saved
  • Good credit (better rate)
  • You plan to stay at least 5 years
  • Total housing cost fits your budget
Your home-buying team

Who does what

RealtorHelps you find and negotiate a home
Loan officerPre-approves you and writes the mortgage
Home inspectorFinds problems before you buy
Escrow officerHolds the money and papers until closing

Fixed vs. ARM

Will your rate change?

Fixed-rate mortgage

Same rate and principal + interest payment for the whole loan.

ARM (adjustable-rate)

Lower rate at first, then it can change; your payment can jump.
