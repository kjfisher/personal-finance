Key vocabulary

Make / modelThe brand (Toyota) / the specific car (Tacoma).
New / used / CPOBrand new · owned before · certified pre-owned: inspected used car with an extra warranty.
Depreciation / appreciationLosing value over time (cars) / gaining value over time (homes usually).
Basic vs. powertrain warrantyCovers most parts for a shorter time / covers engine + transmission for longer.
Pre-approvalA lender tells you how much you can borrow and at what rate before you shop.
Tax, title & registrationAbout 10% added to a car's price in class.
Total interestMonthly payment × number of months − amount borrowed.
Lease / rental applicationThe rental contract / the form a landlord uses to screen you.
Move-in costFirst month + last month + security deposit (in class, a one-month deposit).
2/1, 3/2Bedrooms/bathrooms in a listing.
Down payment / PMICash you pay up front / insurance lenders add if you put down less than 20%.
PITIPrincipal, interest, taxes, insurance: your full monthly house payment.
15- vs. 30-year / ARMShorter term = higher payment, far less interest / adjustable-rate mortgage: rate can change.
EscrowA neutral account that holds money (deposits, taxes, insurance) until it's due.
Cost of attendance (COA)The full yearly cost of college: tuition, fees, housing, food, books, transportation, personal.
Net priceCOA minus grants and scholarships: what you really pay.
FAFSAThe free federal form for college aid (grants, work-study, federal loans). Opens Oct. 1 each year.
Grant / scholarshipFree money you don't pay back (need-based / merit, talent or need).
Subsidized vs. unsubsidized loanSubsidized: the government pays the interest while you're in school. Unsubsidized: interest starts right away.
