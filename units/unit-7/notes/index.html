Marisol T. saved $30,000 and has a steady job. She's buying a $250,000 home with a 15-year mortgage to pay less interest. She's putting ALL $30,000 down: "No emergency fund needed once I own a home."

Claim: smart, not smart, or partly smart? What's smart AND what isn't?
Calculate: her down payment % and 20% of the price
Evidence + Advice (use 2 course words)
Day 31 · page 2

Grade two answers (0, 0.5 or 1 per box)

Sample A: "Smart. Houses go up in value and a 15-year mortgage saves interest, so she's making a good investment."

Sample B: "Partly smart. The 15-year mortgage and steady job are smart, but $30,000 ÷ $250,000 = 12% down, not 20% ($50,000), so she'll pay PMI every month. And using ALL her savings leaves no emergency fund for repairs, which homeowners pay for themselves. She should keep 3–6 months of expenses saved and put the rest down, or wait and save toward 20%."
