Key vocabulary

Gross incomeAll the money you earned (W-2 Box 1 shows wages after pre-tax 401(k)).
Standard deductionAn amount you subtract before figuring tax: $16,100 for a single filer in 2026.
Itemized deductionsListing specific deductions instead; only worth it if they add up to more than the standard deduction.
Taxable incomeBox 1 wages − the standard deduction.
Tax bracketA range of taxable income taxed at one rate. 2026 single: 10% to $12,400, 12% to $50,400, 22% to $105,700.
Progressive taxHigher layers of income are taxed at higher rates; each rate applies only to the dollars in its layer.
Effective tax rateTotal tax ÷ income. Always lower than your top bracket.
W-4The form you fill out at a new job; it sets how much tax is withheld from each paycheck.
W-2The form your employer sends by January 31: last year's wages (Box 1) and tax withheld (Box 2).
1040The federal tax return you file by April 15.
Refund / amount owedWithheld more than your tax = refund. Withheld less = you owe.
Tax creditLowers your tax dollar for dollar. Worth more than a deduction of the same amount.
