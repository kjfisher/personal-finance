Common mistakes · Notes box 4

Bracket traps

  • Subtract the $16,100 FIRST. Brackets use taxable income, not Box 1.
  • Don't multiply ALL income by your top rate. Each rate hits only its layer.
  • The 12% layer starts at $12,400: use (taxable − $12,400) × 12%.
  • Income below $16,100 → taxable income $0 → $0 income tax.
