Saving vs. investing

Where should money live?

Saving

Bank account or CD · very safe · FDIC insured · slow growth · money you need within a few years

Investing

Stocks, bonds, funds · value goes up AND down · can grow much faster · money you won't touch for 5+ years

Two ways to invest · Notes box 2

Be an owner, or be a lender?

Stock = owner

You buy a share, a slice of the company. You earn when the price rises (capital gain) or from a dividend. If the company struggles, your share can fall.

Bond = lender

You loan money to a government or company. It pays you interest, and you get your money back at maturity. Steadier, but slower.
