The advising order · Notes box 3

Fix things in this order

1. Stop the bleedingFees, overdrafts, payday loans, high-interest card debt
2. A starter cushionAbout $1,000 (then 3 months of needs) in an FDIC-insured HYSA
3. Free moneyGet the full 401(k) match
4. Bigger goalsHouse down payment, investing more, paying loans faster
What great advice sounds like

Specific · realistic · backed by a number

  • Weak: "Spend less and save more."
  • Strong: "Cut eating out from $410 to $200 and turn on autopay of $210 to the HYSA: that's $2,520 a year."
  • Same client, different advice is OK, as long as the numbers back it up.
Practice · Notes box 4

Every plan starts with monthly take-home

Paid every 2 weeks: $1,250 a check$1,250 × 26 ÷ 12 = $2,708.33 a month
Paid weekly: $780 a check$780 × 52 ÷ 12 = $3,380.00 a month
Then: money left overMonthly take-home − monthly spending (negative = a shortfall)
Work it in your notes first. Space reveals one line at a time.
Common mistakes · Notes box 4

Advisor traps

  • Biweekly × 2 is NOT monthly: use × 26 ÷ 12 (weekly: × 52 ÷ 12).
  • Budget from NET pay, never gross.
  • Label every number: per check, per month or per year?
  • Advice with no number isn't advice yet.
