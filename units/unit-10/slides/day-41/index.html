Fast math warm-up · Notes box 2

The skills your client needs

Owe $1,500 on a $2,000 limit. Pay off how much to reach 30%?$1,500 − $600 = $900
$1,500 at 24% APR: interest this month?$1,500 × 0.24 ÷ 12 = $30
$50,000 salary, puts in 2%, match up to 5%. Free money missed?3% × $50,000 = $1,500 a year
A $1,800 tax refund is how much a month lent to the IRS?$1,800 ÷ 12 = $150
