Key vocabulary

PremiumWhat you pay for coverage (monthly or yearly), whether you use it or not.
DeductibleWhat you pay before insurance starts paying.
CoinsuranceYour share of the bill after the deductible (e.g., 20%).
CopayA set fee for a visit or prescription (e.g., $30).
Out-of-pocket maxThe most you pay in a year for covered care; then insurance pays 100%.
HMO / PPO / HSAHMO: in-network, need referrals, cheaper · PPO: more choice, costs more · HSA: tax-advantaged savings paired with a high-deductible plan.
LiabilityPays for damage or injuries YOU cause to others. Required to drive in Illinois (25/50/20 minimum).
CollisionPays to fix your car after a crash, no matter who's at fault (minus your deductible).
ComprehensivePays for your car from theft, weather, fire, hitting a deer.
Renters insuranceCovers your belongings and your liability in a rental. The landlord's policy covers only the building.
Life / disability insuranceReplaces income for your family if you die / for you if you can't work.
