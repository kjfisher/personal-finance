Pre-tax 401(k) · Notes box 2

Save for retirement AND pay less income tax now

  • Your 401(k) comes out BEFORE income tax.
  • Taxable pay = gross pay − 401(k).
  • Federal and Illinois tax use taxable pay. Social Security and Medicare still use all of gross pay.
Employer match

Free money: don't leave it on the table

You put in 4%

On $3,600 a month: $144 of your pay goes to your 401(k).

Employer matches 100% up to 4%

Your employer adds another $144. That's $288 a month going to future you.

Worked example · Notes box 3

Jada: $3,600 gross, 5% 401(k)

401(k) 5%$3,600 × 0.05 = $180
Taxable pay$3,600 − $180 = $3,420
Illinois 4.95% (of taxable)$3,420 × 0.0495 = $169.29

Net pay$3,600 − $884.69 = $2,715.31

Training costs · Notes box 5
