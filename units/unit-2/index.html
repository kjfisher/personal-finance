Key vocabulary

Gross payEverything you earn before anything is taken out.
Net payTake-home pay: gross pay minus all deductions.
DeductionMoney taken out of your paycheck (taxes, insurance, retirement).
Mandatory deductionRequired by law: federal income tax, state income tax, Social Security, Medicare.
Optional deductionYour choice: 401(k), health/dental insurance, HSA.
FICASocial Security (6.2%) + Medicare (1.45%) = 7.65% of gross pay.
Illinois income taxA flat 4.95% of taxable pay.
Federal withholdingFederal income tax taken from each check, set by your W-4 and the IRS withholding tables.
401(k) (pre-tax)Retirement savings taken out before income tax, so it lowers taxable pay. FICA still applies.
Employer matchMoney your employer adds to your 401(k) when you contribute. Free money.
Hourly / salary / commissionPaid per hour worked / a set yearly amount / a percent of what you sell.
Overtime1.5 × your hourly rate for hours over 40 in a week (federal law, for hourly workers).
Pay period vs. pay dateThe dates you worked vs. the day you get paid.
YTDYear to date: running total from January 1 through this pay period.
