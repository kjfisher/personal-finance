Debit vs. credit · Notes box 2

Whose money are you spending?

Debit card

Spends YOUR money from checking, right away. Overspend and you may get an overdraft fee.

Credit card

Borrows the BANK's money. You repay later, with interest if you don't pay in full. Builds credit (Unit 6).

What happens when you swipe

Swipe → bank checks your balance → money leaves checking

  • Pending: the purchase is approved but not finished posting
  • Posted: the money is gone from your balance
  • Track it yourself: the app's balance can be behind pending charges

Avoid overdrafts

Four habits

  • Turn on low-balance alerts
  • Check your balance before you buy
  • Opt out of overdraft coverage (the card is declined instead)
  • Link your savings as a backup

Common mistakes · Notes box 5

Checking traps

  • Safe to spend = balance − PENDING charges.
  • Each overdraft gets its own fee, even a $12 one.
  • A minimum-balance fee hits every month you're under, so × 12.
  • Total fees = add EVERY fee on the statement (ATM + overdraft + monthly).
