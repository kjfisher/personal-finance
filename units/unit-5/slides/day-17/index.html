Where can you bank? · Notes box 2

Three kinds of banks

Big bankLots of branches and ATMs; often higher fees, low savings rates
Credit unionNot-for-profit, owned by members; lower fees, better loan rates
Online bankNo branches, so lower costs; usually the highest savings rates
Is my money safe?

FDIC and NCUA insure up to $250,000

  • Per depositor, per bank (FDIC) or credit union (NCUA)
  • If the bank fails, the government covers your insured money
  • NOT covered: stocks, crypto apps, cash at home (no insurance, no interest)

Common mistakes · Notes box 4

CD math traps

  • Compound: each year's % is on the NEW balance, not the original $3,500 (that would be $525, simple interest).
  • Interest earned = ending balance − what you put in.
  • Use 1.05, not 0.05, as the multiplier.
  • Emergency money in a CD? Taking it out early costs a penalty.
