2 · Make the Call (practice)

Tanya B. saved an $8,000 emergency fund. To get the best rate, she put ALL of it in a 5-year CD at 4.6% instead of an HYSA at 4.1%. Taking money out early costs 6 months of interest. Her car now needs a $1,500 repair.

Claim: smart, not smart, or partly smart? What's smart AND what isn't?
Calculate: extra interest per year vs. the early-withdrawal penalty
Evidence + Advice (use 2 course words)
Day 20 · page 2

Grade two answers (0, 0.5 or 1 per box)

Sample A: "Smart. CDs pay more than savings accounts and they are FDIC insured, so her money is safe and growing."

Sample B: "Partly smart. Building an $8,000 emergency fund in an insured account is smart, but locking it in a 5-year CD is not. The CD earns only $8,000 × 0.5% = $40 a year more than the HYSA, but breaking it costs 6 months of interest: $8,000 × 4.6% ÷ 2 = $184. Emergency money has to be easy to reach. She should keep it in an FDIC-insured HYSA and use a CD only for money she won't need."
