5 · Make the Call (practice)

Ari V., 17, saves $40 a month from his job. He keeps $300 for emergencies, has no debt, and leaves everything else in savings at 0.5% "because the stock market is gambling." He'll invest "once I'm 30."

Claim: smart, not smart, or partly smart? What's smart AND what isn't?
Calculate: Rule of 72 at 0.5% vs. an 8% index fund
Evidence + Advice (use 2 course words)

Grade two answers (0, 0.5 or 1 per box)

Sample A: "Ari's plan is not smart. He should invest because compound interest and the Rule of 72 are good. He needs to use the stock market."

Sample B: "Partly smart. Saving $40 a month and keeping $300 for emergencies is smart, but leaving long-term money at 0.5% and waiting until 30 is not. 72 ÷ 0.5 = 144 years to double vs. 72 ÷ 8 = 9 years, so waiting 13 years costs about 1.5 doubles. Keep the $300 in savings and put the $40 a month in a low-fee index fund in a Roth IRA now, because his long time horizon lets compound interest work."
