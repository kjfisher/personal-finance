Key vocabulary

BudgetA plan for how you'll use your take-home pay each month.
Need / wantNeed: required to live and work (housing, food, transportation). Want: nice to have.
Fixed expenseThe same amount every month (rent, phone plan, car payment).
Variable expenseChanges month to month (groceries, gas, eating out).
Unexpected expenseA surprise cost (car repair, medical bill, broken phone).
Zero-based budgetEvery dollar of take-home pay gets a job: income − expenses − savings = $0.
50/30/20A guideline: 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings and extra debt payments.
Savings abilityTake-home pay − expenses. What's left to save each month.
Emergency fundSavings for surprises: start with $1,000, build to 3–6 months of expenses.
Short / mid / long-term goalUnder 1 year / 1–5 years / 5+ years.
Trade-offWhat you give up when you choose one option over another (opportunity cost).
