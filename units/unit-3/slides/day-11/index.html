Two budgeting tools · Notes box 2

Zero-based vs. 50/30/20

Zero-based budget

Every dollar of take-home pay gets a job. Take-home − expenses − savings = $0. Nothing is "left over" by accident.

50/30/20 guideline

A quick check: about 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings and extra debt payments.

Pay yourself first

Savings is a line in your budget, not leftovers

  • Set up an automatic transfer on payday.
  • What's left after savings is what you can spend.
  • Weekly costs → monthly: × 52 ÷ 12 (not × 4, which misses about 4 weeks a year).

Common mistakes · Notes box 3

Budget math traps

  • Weekly × 4 is NOT monthly. Use × 52 ÷ 12.
  • Expenses bigger than take-home = a SHORTFALL, not savings ability.
  • Zero-based doesn't mean saving $0. It means $0 left WITHOUT a job.
  • 50/30/20 uses TAKE-HOME pay, not gross.
