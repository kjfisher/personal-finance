Needs vs. wants · Notes box 2

What do you really need?

Need

Required to live and work: housing, basic food, utilities, transportation to work, basic phone service.

Want

Makes life nicer but you could live without it: eating out, the newest phone, streaming, concerts.

18 by 18 #3

Spend on what matters to YOU

  • Some things are gray: a car is a need if there's no bus, a want if it's a luxury model.
  • Your values decide the gray areas: friends, family, health, a goal you care about.
  • Spending on what matters isn't "never spend." It's choosing on purpose.

Three kinds of expenses · Notes box 3

Fixed, variable, unexpected

Fixed: same amount every monthRent, phone plan, car payment, insurance
Variable: changes month to monthGroceries, gas, eating out, electricity
Unexpected: surprise costsCar repair, medical bill, broken phone
18 by 18 #4

Expect the unexpected

  • Unexpected expenses WILL happen; you just don't know when.
  • A budget needs a cushion, and you need an emergency fund.
  • Start with $1,000, then build to 3–6 months of expenses.
