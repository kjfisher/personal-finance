Key vocabulary

CreditBorrowing money now and promising to pay it back later.
Credit score (FICO)A 300–850 number that tells lenders how likely you are to repay. 670+ is good; 800+ is exceptional.
Payment history (35%)Paying on time, every time. The biggest part of your score.
Amounts owed (30%)How much you owe, especially utilization: balance ÷ limit. Keep it under 30%.
Length of credit history (15%)How long your accounts have been open.
New credit (10%) / credit mix (10%)Recent applications (hard inquiries) / having different kinds of credit.
Credit bureausExperian, Equifax and TransUnion keep your credit reports.
Credit reportYour borrowing record. Free at annualcreditreport.com.
Hard vs. soft inquiryApplying for credit (can lower your score a little) vs. checking your own score (no effect).
APRThe yearly interest rate on borrowed money. Monthly interest ≈ balance × APR ÷ 12.
Secured card / authorized user / cosignerWays to start credit: a deposit-backed card / using someone's account / someone promises to repay if you don't.
Revolving vs. installmentCredit cards (borrow, repay, borrow again) vs. loans with set payments (car, mortgage).
