Extension (optional) · How card companies really do it

Average daily balance: timing matters

Days 1–10 owe $1,200 · Days 11–20 owe $1,800 · Days 21–30 owe $90030-day cycle, 22% APR
Average daily balance($1,200×10 + $1,800×10 + $900×10) ÷ 30 = $1,300
Interest = ADB × APR ÷ 365 × days$1,300 × 0.22 ÷ 365 × 30 ≈ $23.51
Same math on the $900 ending balance$16.50, so paying early in the cycle saves money
