2 · Make the Call (practice)

Jalen W., 18, opened a $500 secured card and pays it IN FULL every month. He puts about $400 a month on it (gas + food). His dad also made him an authorized user on a card that's often paid late. "I'm doing everything right."

Claim: smart, not smart, or partly smart? What's smart AND what isn't?
Calculate: his utilization and the most he should charge for 30%
Evidence + Advice (use 2 course words)
Day 26 · page 2

Grade two answers (0, 0.5 or 1 per box)

Sample A: "Smart. He pays in full so he never pays interest. Credit cards build credit, so his score will go up fast."

Sample B: "Partly smart. The secured card and paying in full are smart (payment history 35%), but $400 ÷ $500 = 80% utilization hurts amounts owed (30%). To stay at 30% he should charge no more than $500 × 0.30 = $150. His dad's late payments also show on his report. He should keep only his $40 phone bill on the card, pay in full, and ask to be removed as an authorized user."
